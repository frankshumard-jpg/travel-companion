Arrival Day Logistics

Travel-day checklist from the United States to Heathrow and onward to The May Fair Hotel.

Flight and Arrival Overview
United Airlines Flight Information
  • Carrier: United Airlines
  • Travel day focus: Long-haul US departure to London Heathrow (LHR)
  • Arrival target: Heathrow Airport around midday local time
  • Reminder: Confirm terminal, baggage policy, and seat assignments in the United app before boarding
Heathrow Arrival Steps
  1. Follow signs to UK Border / Immigration.
  2. Keep passports and landing details ready for inspection.
  3. Proceed to baggage claim once cleared.
  4. After baggage collection, continue to Customs exit and arrivals hall.
Airport to Hotel Transfer
Immigration, Customs, and Baggage Reminders
  • Immigration: Have passport and any required travel documentation immediately available.
  • Customs: Keep declarations accurate and accessible before you reach the inspection point.
  • Baggage claim: Check monitors for your belt number and verify every checked bag before exit.
  • Delayed baggage: Report missing bags at the airline desk before leaving the secure area.
Private Transfer to The May Fair Hotel
  • Pickup point: Heathrow arrivals hall (as directed by transfer provider)
  • Estimated ride time: 45-90 minutes depending on traffic
  • Driver coordination: Keep phone on and monitor messages after clearing Customs
  • Contingency: If you cannot find the driver, contact the transfer company first, then hotel concierge

Arrival Day Practical Reminders
Money, Phone, and Power
  • Currency: Keep a small amount of GBP available for immediate expenses.
  • SIM/eSIM: Activate roaming or eSIM profile before leaving the airport.
  • Electrical adapter: UK Type G plug required; confirm you packed adapter and charging cables.
Time Difference and Jet Lag
  • Time shift: London time is significantly ahead of most US time zones.
  • Jet lag strategy: Hydrate, stay active through early evening, and avoid long naps on arrival day.
  • Sleep target: Aim for a normal local bedtime to reset quickly.
First-Day Tips After Check-In
  • Secure passports and valuables in-room before heading out.
  • Take a short neighborhood walk only if energy allows.
  • Keep the evening light and focus on rest for the next day.
Nearby Essentials Around The May Fair
Nearby Essentials Around The May Fair
  • ATM: Cash points available around Piccadilly and Green Park station area.
  • Pharmacy: Boots and Superdrug locations within central Mayfair.
  • Groceries: Marks and Spencer Food Hall and local convenience stores nearby.
