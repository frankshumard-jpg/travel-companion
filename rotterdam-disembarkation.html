Overview

Disembarkation city: Rotterdam is the final port of call and the place where the cruise ends.

Local feel: The city is a modern port hub with efficient terminal services and a smooth transfer process toward Amsterdam Airport Schiphol.

Terminal note: Holland America handles the disembarkation flow, porter services, and the transfer to the airport.

Plan Your Visit
  • First steps: Follow the ship's instructions for luggage tags, departure time, and designated shuttle boarding point.
  • Timing: Arrive at the terminal area with documents ready and luggage tickets in hand.
  • Process: Expect clear directions once the ship docks.
  • Support: Ask staff immediately if anything is unclear before moving to transfer area.
Getting There
  • Disembark in assigned group and move to porter/coach staging area.
  • Keep passport, ticket, and customs paperwork easily reachable.
  • Confirm transfer coach location and boarding time as soon as you leave ship.

Overview

Transportation: Holland America provides transfer from the Rotterdam cruise terminal to Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS).

Travel time: Usually around 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Plan Your Visit
  • Departure: Exact pickup time is assigned by Holland America.
  • Boarding: Be ready in transfer area well before assigned time.

  • Arrival: Coach drop-off at Schiphol for direct flight processing.
Getting There
  • Move from terminal check-out area to assigned HAL coach lane.

  • Remain seated with documents accessible during transfer.

Overview

Arrival at AMS: Schiphol is large, so allow time from coach drop-off to airline check-in.

Check-in: Locate United/Air Canada desks and finalize boarding passes if needed.

Security: Expect possible queues; keep liquids/electronics ready.

Plan Your Visit
  • Terminal layout: Well signposted, but allow extra walking time.
  • Rest stops: Use cafes/lounges when timing allows before long-haul flights.
  • Train Information: Schiphol has an integrated rail station if backup routing is needed.
  • Taxi Information: Official taxi stands are available near arrivals/departures if required.
Getting There
  • Follow check-in signage immediately after coach drop-off.
  • Use train station only if you need route flexibility or onward surface transport.
  • Use official taxi queue only; avoid unmarked solicitors.
Nearby Attractions
  • Schiphol rail platforms Inside airport complex
  • Official taxi rank Follow airport signs
Travel Information

Travel Tips
