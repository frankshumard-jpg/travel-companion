The Newest Chapter in Lynn and Frank’s Cruise Adventure

Well, apparently just taking a cruise isn’t enough anymore.

For the past two weeks, I’ve been buried in maps, ports, trains, pubs, attractions, directions, photographs, cruise terminals and enough computer code to make me question several of my recent life choices. The result is the beginning of something new for Beyond the Gangway Travel.

Before anybody gets excited, no, Lynn and I are not travel agents, and we do not own a travel agency. Not yet, anyway. That particular adventure is still somewhere on the drawing board. But we have spent an unreasonable amount of our lives traveling, cruising, getting lost, finding our way again, and occasionally discovering that getting lost was the best part of the day.

So, as a courtesy to everyone sailing with us through England, Scotland, Ireland, the Netherlands and Norway, we’re opening up a sample of something I’ve been working on for the past couple of weeks.

It is our Beyond the Gangway Travel Companion, and this is very much the Beta version. Think of it as a test drive. It already includes our ports, attractions, maps, walking directions from wherever you happen to be standing, transportation information, nearby sights, planning tools, places to eat and drink, photo storage, and a travel journal. And we’re just getting started.

Eventually, the idea is to turn this into a much more complete travel app—something you can carry on your phone that acts like your own personal travel advisor when you walk off the ship. Instead of wondering Where are we? How do we get there? What’s nearby? How do we get back? And is there a decent pub anywhere around here? you pull out your phone and have the answers in your pocket.

Hopefully, sometime in the next few days I’ll also figure out how to make it so you can load it onto your iPhone or iPad and use it like an app instead of simply opening a website every time. That’s the plan, anyway. Sorry, Android folks—I don’t have it ready for Android phones yet. Give me a little time. I’ve only been pretending I know how to build an app for two weeks.

For now, this is a sample. Play with it. Try it on the trip. Push the buttons. See what works. If something doesn’t, remember that the technical department currently consists largely of one aging bald guy staring at a computer and occasionally threatening it.

This is only the beginning of the next chapter of Lynn and Frank’s Cruise Adventure.
