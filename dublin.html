Transportation & Getting Around
Cruise Port Transfer Information
  • Taxi/shuttle to city center is usually most efficient.
  • Allow for port movement and city traffic.
  • Set first attraction before leaving port.
Getting into Dublin
  • Fastest: direct taxi to first stop.
  • Budget: bus/public transit from port approaches.
  • Use a fixed first destination to avoid drift.
Walking Strategy
  • Best central triangle: Trinity-Castle-Temple Bar.
  • Stay in one cluster at a time.
  • Budget extra time for crossings and intersections.
Taxi Guidance
  • Useful for port leg, weather shifts, and timing recovery.
  • Availability is generally good centrally.
  • Keep taxi fallback for return window.
Public Transportation
  • Buses and LUAS support cross-city moves.
  • Use transit for one long jump, then walk clusters.
  • Avoid frequent mode changes mid-route.
Return-to-Ship Timing
  • Hard rule: Start return 2 hours before all-aboard.
  • Add 20-30 minute traffic margin.
  • Keep direct taxi fallback ready.
